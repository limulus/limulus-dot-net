It’s always struck me as a bit weird that we effectively treat Independence Day as the USA’s
birthday. Sure, it’s an important date in the country’s history, but the signing of the
Constitution is where our nation really got its start. What came before it is unrecognizable
to us as our country.

Any way, today is a good day to watch [this video][link] and be reminded that change is
possible and that revolutions don’t always require blood. Even the Constitution can be
amended.
